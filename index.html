Why B&B

A taxi you can
plan around.

Most of our work comes from repeat customers and word of mouth. That only happens when the car turns up when it should and the fare is what we said.

  • On time, every time

    The driver leaves early enough to absorb Calicut traffic. For flights and trains we build in a buffer.

  • Fare agreed before you go

    You get the number up front. Tolls, parking and permits at actuals — nothing invented at the end.

  • Drivers who know the roads

    Licensed, experienced and local. They know the ghat roads, the shortcuts and where to stop for a good meal.

  • Clean, well-kept cars

    Serviced on schedule, cleaned inside and out before each trip, air conditioning that actually works.

  • Reachable at any hour

    Calls and WhatsApp messages answered around the clock, including for 3am airport pickups.

  • Ready for the long haul

    All-Kerala and interstate permits in order, so a Bengaluru or Mysuru run is no different to a city trip.
